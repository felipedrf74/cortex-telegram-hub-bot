## Data We Process

Nexus Hub may process account identifiers, authentication records, app usage events, chat and task content, calendar/task provider metadata, health and fitness summaries, finance entries, content planning data, push-notification tokens, subscription records, audit logs, and support communications.

## Why We Process It

We use this data to provide the product, authenticate users, sync connected services, generate user-requested plans or summaries, operate billing and entitlements, secure the service, diagnose reliability issues, and satisfy legal obligations.

## Connected Providers

Users choose whether to connect third-party services such as Google, Outlook, Garmin, Stripe, Apple, and push-notification providers. Provider tokens are stored server-side only when needed for the integration and are removed locally during account deletion where technically possible.

## Retention And Deletion

Users can request export and deletion from in-app settings. Product data is deleted from user-owned tables. Audit records may be retained where needed to prove export, consent, deletion, security, or legal events.

## International Processing

Nexus Hub may process data in infrastructure, monitoring, email, payment, and provider systems outside the user's country. Counsel must confirm publication wording, transfer mechanisms, subprocessors, and regional disclosures.

## Contact

Privacy and support requests should go to support@nexushub.me.
