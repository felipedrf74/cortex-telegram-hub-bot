## Controller And Contact

Nexus Hub is operated by Felipe Dominguez in Portugal. Privacy, export, deletion, and support requests go to support@nexushub.me.

## Data We Process

Nexus Hub may process account identifiers, authentication records, onboarding preferences, language, user profile/focus, app usage events, chat and task content, notes, calendar events, calendar/task/email provider metadata, health and fitness summaries, readiness, workouts, sleep, finance entries, receipts, manually entered transactions, content planning data, recipes, push-notification tokens, subscription and billing records, audit logs, diagnostics, and support communications.

## Why We Process It

We use this data to provide the product, authenticate users, sync connected services, run the Nexus Hub orchestration engine, generate requested plans or summaries, surface Decision Center items, operate billing and entitlements, secure the service, diagnose reliability issues, prevent abuse, provide support, and satisfy legal obligations. Nexus Hub does not sell personal data or use it for behavioral advertising.

## Connected Providers And Subprocessors

Users choose whether to connect third-party services such as Google, Microsoft, Garmin, Apple Health, Strava, Stripe, App Store payment services, email providers, push-notification providers, hosting, monitoring, and model providers. Provider tokens are stored server-side only when needed for the integration and should be removed during account deletion where technically possible. Model providers are used only when a requested feature needs that processing; user data is not used to train third-party models.

## Retention, Export, And Deletion

Users can request export and deletion from in-app settings or support. Product data is deleted from user-owned tables where technically possible. Audit records may be retained where needed to prove export, consent, deletion, security, billing, tax, or legal events.

## Security

Nexus Hub uses encryption in transit, protected storage, access controls, minimized/redacted logs, and secure token handling. Counsel should review public wording for exact encryption claims and subprocessors before publication.

## International Processing

Nexus Hub may process data in infrastructure, monitoring, email, payment, model-provider, and provider systems outside the user's country, including the EU, Brazil, and the United States. Counsel must confirm transfer mechanisms, subprocessors, and regional disclosures.

## User Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, users may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or complaint with a supervisory authority.

## Children

The product is not intended for children under 16.
