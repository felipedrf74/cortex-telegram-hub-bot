# Marathon Periodization Notes

- Base: grow easy aerobic frequency and long-run durability.
- Build: introduce threshold and marathon-specific long-run progression.
- Peak: preserve long-run quality, reduce non-essential strength, avoid extra fatigue.
- Taper: keep frequency, reduce volume, maintain a small amount of quality.
- Race week: no new stimulus, no make-up intensity, keep the athlete fresh and confident.
