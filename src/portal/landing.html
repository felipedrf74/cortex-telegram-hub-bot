FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Missing something? Email felipe@nexushub.me — the founder reads every message.

What does "pre-alpha" mean for me?

It means Nexus Hub is running in production for a tiny group of early users and it works — but the feature set is still being shaped. Pre-alpha users get: (a) a lifetime price lock at founder pricing, (b) a direct line to the founder, (c) heavy influence on which integrations ship next.

What it doesn't mean: bugs that corrupt your data. All sync is one-way-read-only to start, then write-back is gated behind an explicit setting per provider.

Do I have to move off Todoist/Notion/my calendar?

No. That's the whole point. Nexus Hub is the AI layer above your existing tools. You keep using Todoist for the quick-add keyboard shortcut you love. You keep using Notion for the second-brain setup you've tuned. You keep using Google Calendar for the invite flow.

Nexus Hub reads from all of them, reasons across them, and writes changes back to the one you pick as your default. If you cancel Nexus Hub tomorrow, all your data stays exactly where it was.

Why is this better than Motion, Reclaim, or Sunsama?

Those tools are great at what they do, but they all live in a single domain — calendar and tasks. They don't know your HRV is low, they don't know you have content filming Thursday, they don't know your deadline just moved.

Nexus Hub's differentiator is cross-domain reasoning: the AI sees your fitness readiness, your content pipeline, your finances, and your calendar at the same time, and the recommendations come from intersecting all of them. "Move the hard workout to Thursday" isn't a calendar optimization — it's a fitness decision informed by content deadlines.

I have ADHD. Will this actually help or just add another app?

Honest answer: it's designed for you specifically. The core insight is that ADHD makes you pay a "context-switch tax" every time you open a new app to check on something. Nexus Hub collapses all those checks into one chat interface (either iOS app or Telegram bot, your choice) so the 17 times a day you ask "what am I supposed to be doing right now?" become one place to ask, not twelve.

The daily context block also means the AI doesn't need to be reminded who you are every conversation. It already knows.

What data do you store? Is this secure?

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with per-user AES-256 keys. Your task/calendar/wearable data lives in a local SQLite database on the server and is never sold, shared, or used to train any model. The AI itself runs through Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) under Nexus Hub's own API keys — your data isn't sent to them for training purposes under their enterprise terms.

You can export your entire dataset as JSON via the admin portal and delete your account with one click. No dark patterns.

Is there an iOS app? Android?

Native iOS app is in beta now (SwiftUI, iOS 17+). Android is planned for after alpha — if you want it sooner, say so in your waitlist signup. You can also use Nexus Hub entirely through the Telegram bot if you prefer text.
