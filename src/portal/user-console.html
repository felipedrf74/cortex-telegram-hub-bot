Secretary

Scheduling, priorities, routines — the background operator of your day.

What Secretary does

Secretary reads your calendar(s), applies your priority rules, and protects focus blocks. It hands context to other skills (Training for scheduling, Content for publish cadence).

Calendar integrations live on the Integrations page. Routines and priority rules are edited from the iOS app; editing them here is tracked as an open item.

Training

Fitness goals, schedule, readiness. Being split into gym / running / cycle / swim in Phase 1.

What Training does

Training merges Garmin/Strava/Whoop telemetry with Secretary's schedule awareness to recommend sessions, warn about overreach, and guide recovery.

Phase 1 will break Training into sub-skills (gym / running / cycle / swim). When that ships, each sub-skill gets its own sidebar entry here.

Finance

Budget awareness, categorization, affordability context for other skills.

What Finance does

Finance keeps a lightweight model of your budget and categories. It answers "can I afford this?" for other skills (Cooking meal cost, Training gear purchases).

Budget editing lives in the iOS app; this console currently shows read-only status.

Cooking

Meal planning, dietary preferences, kitchen cost awareness.

What Cooking does

Cooking plans meals against your dietary rules, pantry state, and (optionally) your Finance budget. When Training nutrition is needed, it syncs with Training's session plan.
